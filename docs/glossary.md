# Glossary of the semantic model of protein function prediction

Version 0.2.0. Generated by `scripts/build.py` from `data/concepts.yaml`; do not edit by hand.

A concept marked accepted carries the canonical name the author chose; one marked candidate is not named yet. A row with several candidate names groups concepts that still have to be split.

## adoption rule

- **Identifier:** `adoption_rule` (accepted)
- **Spanish label:** regla de adopción
- **Top category:** method
- **Values:** `for`, `against`, `equal`, `below_effect`, `underpowered`

An arm replaces its reference in the recipe when some cell is resolved in its favour by the simultaneous intervals with an estimated gain of at least 0.01, and no cell is resolved against it (the author, 2026-11-27). The family is always the nine cells; a verdict never deletes an arm.

Names today: PROTEA, accepted names: adoption_rule, one_cell_at_effect_none_against, below_effect.

## calibrated maximum

## calibration stratum

- **Identifier:** `calibration_stratum` (accepted)
- **Spanish label:** estrato de calibración
- **Top category:** data

## coverage guarantee

- **Identifier:** `coverage_guarantee` (accepted)
- **Spanish label:** garantía de cobertura
- **Top category:** method

The recipe's last step: per protein and aspect, when no candidate GO term not yet known reaches the declared threshold, the best one is raised to it and flagged forced; when there is none, the best GO term of the frequency set is forced instead and flagged reserve. Adoption comparisons read arms without it, and its cost is its own declared comparison.

Names today: PROTEA, accepted names: apply_coverage_guarantee, raise_best_unknown_term_to_declared_threshold, forced, reserve, delivered_reading.

## literature ladder

- **Identifier:** `literature_ladder` (accepted)
- **Spanish label:** escalera de la literatura
- **Top category:** activity
